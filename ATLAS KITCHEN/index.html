What We Offer

PASTILLA

A perfect harmony of savory spiced chicken and sweet almonds wrapped in layers of crispy golden pastry.

TANJIA

A Marrakech specialty of tender meat slow-cooked in a traditional clay urn with preserved lemon, garlic, and cumin.

COUSCOUS

The iconic national dish featuring fluffy steamed semolina topped with succulent meat and a medley of fresh garden vegetables in a rich broth.

About Our Restaurant

"Welcome to Atlas Kitchen, where every dish tells a story. We bring the authentic flavors of Morocco straight to your table, using time-honored recipes and the freshest local ingredients. From aromatic tagines to fluffy couscous, experience a culinary journey that warms the heart and delights the senses."

Our mission is to bring the authentic warmth and flavors of Moroccan hospitality to your table.
